2. Description of Service

Claritly is an AI-powered writing assistant designed as a Chrome extension. It allows users to select text on webpages and rewrite it into various tones (e.g., Professional, Friendly, Short). The service requires Google authentication to manage user accounts and usage limits.

3. How Claritly Works

Claritly operates uniquely compared to standard web applications:

  • Selection Based: The extension only activates when you intentionally highlight text and click the Claritly popup icon.
  • In-Place Editing: When you choose to rewrite text, Claritly attempts to replace the selected text directly within the webpage's input field.
  • No Background Monitoring: Claritly does not actively monitor your typing, read your entire screen, or track your keystrokes. It only processes the specific text you submit for rewriting.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

TL;DR: We only process the text you actively highlight and choose to rewrite. We do not track your keystrokes, we do not read your general browsing data, and your rewritten text is never used to train AI models.

At Claritly, your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Chrome extension and website.

1. What Information We Collect

Account Information

To use Claritly, you must sign in using Google Authentication. We collect basic profile information provided by Google, specifically your email address, to create and manage your account, verify your subscription status, and enforce usage limits (e.g., free tier allowances).

Usage Data (Text Processing)

When you highlight text and request a rewrite, that specific text snippet is sent securely to our backend servers to be processed by our AI models (via secure APIs). We do not save, store, or log the text you send us for processing. Once the AI generates the rewritten text and sends it back to your browser, the data is discarded from our active memory.

2. What We DO NOT Collect

  • No Keystroke Logging: We do not record or monitor what you type.
  • No Screen Reading: We do not scan or read the contents of the webpages you visit. The extension only "sees" the exact text you highlight and explicitly click to rewrite.
  • No Password Fields: The extension is technically restricted from activating on password fields, hidden fields, and read-only fields for your security.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your email address solely for:

  • Authenticating your secure login.
  • Tracking your usage limits (Free tier vs Premium tier).
  • Sending essential service updates or payment-related communication.

4. Your Data is NEVER Used for Training

The text you rewrite using Claritly is strictly confidential. We have strict agreements with our AI providers (like DeepSeek and OpenRouter) ensuring that your input data is NOT used to train, improve, or fine-tune their AI models. Your data is processed temporarily and immediately discarded.

5. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services to operate Claritly securely:

  • Supabase: Used for secure database hosting and Google Authentication handling.
  • Gumroad: Used for secure payment processing. We do not see or store your credit card details.
  • AI Providers: We route requests through secure APIs (DeepSeek, OpenRouter) exclusively for generating text.

6. Data Security

All communication between the Claritly Chrome extension, our servers, and third-party APIs is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS protocols. We implement strict security measures to protect your account information.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated email address from our database at any time. Simply contact our support team, and we will remove your account.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at: support@claritly.ai

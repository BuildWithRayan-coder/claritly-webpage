Privacy Policy — Claritly

Last Updated: August 14, 2026

Claritly is a Chrome extension designed to help users choose the right tone and make their messages clearer, more specific, and more effective. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Beta Notice

Claritly is currently in beta / testing phase. Features, functionality, and this Privacy Policy may change as we continue to improve the product. We encourage users to check back periodically for updates.

2. Information We Collect

To provide and manage your account, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Other minor account-related details (such as basic usage or preference settings) necessary to operate the extension

We only collect what is needed to run the service — nothing more.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide customer support
  • Improve the general functionality and reliability of Claritly

4. Your Data Is Never Used for Training

We want to be clear and direct about this:

  • We do not use your data, messages, or chats for AI training purposes — at all.
  • Your conversations and content processed through Claritly are not used to train, fine-tune, or update any AI models.
  • Your personal data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for training or advertising purposes.

5. Data Storage & Security

We store only the minimal information described in Section 2 (name, email, and basic account details). We take reasonable measures to protect this information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

6. Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to:

  • Request access to the data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Ask questions about how your information is handled

7. Changes to This Policy

Because Claritly is in active beta development, this Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the extension after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

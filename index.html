Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Claritly.

Is Claritly free to use, or will I be asked to pay later?
Claritly offers your first 50 rewrites completely free of charge. Once you've experienced the value, you can seamlessly upgrade to a premium plan for unlimited access and advanced features.
Does the extension read or store my private browsing data?
Absolutely not. Claritly prioritizes your privacy. We do not read your general browsing data or store your keystrokes. We only process the specific text you actively highlight and choose to rewrite.
What types of content can I rewrite with Claritly?
Claritly works wherever you type. You can enhance any text input on the web, including emails, direct messages, social media comments, and live chat platforms.
Does it automatically change text on my screen, or do I stay in control?
You are always in full control. Claritly never alters your text automatically; it only updates the specific text you select and approve for rewriting.
Will using this tool slow down my web browser or site loading speed?
Not at all. Claritly is designed to be exceptionally lightweight (just a few megabytes) and highly optimized. It operates seamlessly in the background without impacting your browser's performance or page load speeds.
